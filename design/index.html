Design Direction

Infra Luxury: clean, technical, and unmistakably premium

This is the visual contract for investor-facing Prodigy surfaces. We combine infrastructure credibility with cinematic polish so the product feels both reliable and category-defining.

Core Principles

Infra clarity first

Every section answers one question: signal in, decision out, code shipped. No decorative ambiguity.

Premium restraint

Strong type, careful spacing, and sparse accent color. Feels expensive without being loud.

Operational confidence

Show states, status, and flow. Visual language should feel like a command center, not a brochure.

Proof over promises

Prefer measured outcomes, architecture views, and process traces to generic marketing claims.

Motion Rules
