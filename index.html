Convertible roles

One AI employee. Three operating modes.

Prodigy changes posture based on the team's gap: planning, implementation, support, handoff, or overnight ownership.

Product Manager

Intake, prioritize, delegate

Runs planning from live context, ranks backlog items, assigns owners, and posts PM digests with rationale.

Senior Engineer

Implement, review, unblock

Takes critical bugs, writes code, opens PRs, reviews junior work, and escalates only when human judgment is needed.

Junior Engineer

Execute assigned tickets

Handles scoped tasks, posts status updates, raises blockers, and builds confidence through visible evidence.
